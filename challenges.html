Trading Rules

The Rules, In Plain Words

Everything that can end a challenge is on this list. Nothing else will.

  • Profit target — 2-Step: 8% in Phase 1, then 5% in Phase 2. 1-Step: 8% in one phase. Instant: 8% growth target. Reach it without breaking a loss limit to pass.
  • Daily loss limit — 4% — lose more than 4% in one day and the attempt ends. The limit resets each day.
  • Maximum loss — 8% — the account may never fall more than 8% below its starting balance.
  • Minimum trading days — 3 — every route needs at least 3 trading days. There is no maximum time limit.
  • Leverage — 2-Step 1:100, 1-Step 1:50, Instant 1:30.
  • Fee refund — 100% (2-Step & 1-Step) — your challenge fee is refunded in full when you pass and become funded. Instant Funding has no refund — there is no challenge to pass.
  • Allowed — news trading, EAs, scalping and hedging, within the loss limits.
  • Not allowed — account sharing, copying another trader's account, or exploiting platform errors.
