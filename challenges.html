The Rules, In Plain Words

Everything that can end a challenge is on this list. Nothing else will.

  • Daily loss limit — lose more than the daily limit in one day and the attempt ends. The limit resets each day.
  • Maximum loss — the account may never fall more than 8% below its starting balance.
  • Profit target — reach the target without breaking a loss limit to pass a phase.
  • Allowed — news trading, EAs, scalping and hedging, within the loss limits.
  • Not allowed — account sharing, copying another trader's account, or exploiting platform errors.
